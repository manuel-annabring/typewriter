Typewriter sound clips for the typewriter site.

All source recordings are licensed Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0, public domain
dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution
is required; the authors are listed here as a record. The licence was read on
each Freesound page on 2026-10-30.

Processing (ffmpeg): the clips come from Freesound's HQ preview MP3s (the
original files need a login), cut to a single hit, mixed to mono, short fades
at the ends, peak-normalised to -3 dB (return_slide shares return_stop's gain
so the slide stays quieter than the end-stop), re-encoded as mono 44.1 kHz MP3
(LAME -q:a 2).

                  https://freesound.org/people/yottasounds/sounds/380138/
key_2.mp3         "Typewriter - single key - type 2.wav" by yottasounds, whole clip
                  https://freesound.org/people/yottasounds/sounds/380137/
space_1.mp3       "Silver Typewriter (Space Bar)" by moodyfingers, 0.375 to 0.575 s
                  https://freesound.org/people/moodyfingers/sounds/734318/
bell.mp3          "Typewriter Bell.wav" by ramsamba, whole clip
                  https://freesound.org/people/ramsamba/sounds/318687/
return_slide.mp3  "Typewriter bell & carriage reset" by knufds, 1.69 to 2.37 s
return_stop.mp3   same file, 2.385 to 2.785 s (the end-stop thud)
                  https://freesound.org/people/knufds/sounds/345955/
return_full.mp3   "Typewriter Carriage Return.wav" by ramsamba, 0 to 0.8 s
                  (an alternative to the two-part return above)
                  https://freesound.org/people/ramsamba/sounds/318686/

Shift, Tab, Backspace, the margin-lock clunk and the lever press have no clips
of their own: the engine plays the key clips lower and slower (playbackRate).

paper_out.mp3     same file, 100.05 to 101.3 s (paper wound out)
                  https://freesound.org/people/keithpeter/sounds/123344/
                  CC0, licence read on the Freesound page on 2026-10-31. The recording is quiet, so
                  these two are high-passed at 80 Hz and boosted to -3 dB peak (hiss is audible).
